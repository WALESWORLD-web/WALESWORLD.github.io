Top 10 Football Players to Watch This Season

September 30, 2026

Football continues to produce exciting players who attract millions of fans around the world. Here are ten players to watch closely this season.

1. Kylian Mbappé

Mbappé remains one of the world's most exciting attacking players, known for his pace, finishing and ability to create dangerous chances.

2. Erling Haaland

Haaland is known for his powerful finishing, movement and goal-scoring ability.

3. Lamine Yamal

The young Spanish winger has attracted worldwide attention with his technical ability, creativity and confidence.

4. Vinícius Júnior

Vinícius continues to be an important attacking player, combining speed, dribbling and creativity.

5. Jude Bellingham

Bellingham has developed into an influential midfielder capable of contributing both defensively and in attack.

6. Bukayo Saka

Saka remains an important attacking player, contributing goals, assists and creativity.

7. Jamal Musiala

Musiala is known for his close control, dribbling and ability to create opportunities in attacking areas.

8. Florian Wirtz

Wirtz has established himself as a creative midfielder with the ability to create chances and score goals.

9. Rodri

Rodri is recognised for his passing, positioning and ability to control the midfield.

10. Mohamed Salah

Salah continues to be a major attacking threat, known for his goals, assists and consistency.
