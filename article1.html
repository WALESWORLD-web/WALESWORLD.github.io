Top 10 Football Players to Watch This Season

September 30, 2026

Football continues to attract millions of fans around the world, with talented players competing at the highest level.

This season, several players are expected to attract significant attention because of their performances, goals, assists and influence on their teams.

1. Kylian Mbappé

The French forward remains one of the biggest names in world football. His speed, finishing and ability to create chances make him an important player to watch.

2. Erling Haaland

Haaland is known for his powerful finishing and goal-scoring ability. His physical strength and positioning make him a constant threat.

3. Lamine Yamal

The young Spanish winger has attracted worldwide attention with his technical ability, creativity and confidence on the ball.

4. Jude Bellingham

Bellingham combines midfield ability with attacking contributions and has become an important player at the highest level.

5. Vinícius Júnior

The Brazilian forward is known for his pace, dribbling and ability to create opportunities in the final third.

6. Harry Kane

Kane continues to be recognised for his finishing, passing and ability to contribute both goals and assists.

7. Bukayo Saka

The English winger has developed into an important attacking player, combining creativity with goals and assists.

8. Jamal Musiala

Musiala is known for his close control, dribbling and ability to operate between midfield and attack.

9. Florian Wirtz

Wirtz has established himself as a creative attacking midfielder capable of producing goals, assists and important moments.

10. Victor Osimhen

The Nigerian striker is known for his pace, strength and finishing ability, making him a player many football supporters follow closely.
